New paper discussing traveling fronts in quenched disordered media [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14914v1) ! We show numerically that random disorder accelerates the wave with respect to deterministic case. 
The exact distribution of the disorder does not seem to matter beyond its first two moments.
