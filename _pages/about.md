I am a PhD student in the [Mathematics department](https://www.maths.unitn.it/) of the University of Trento and in the [Complex Human Behavior Lab](https://chub.fbk.eu/) in FBK. I have also spent time at [CASA](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/bartlett/casa), in University College London. My main research topic is the growth of cities. I combine theoretical modeling, empirical analysis, and numerical simulations to study these processes, drawing on tools from statistical physics. I have worked on (1) data analysis to characterize city growth via empirical regularities (2) surface growth, empirical measures and polydisperse deposition simulations (3) traveling fronts in quenched media (4) first-passage percolation (5) random matrix theory (6) developing a science of city.

I am currently looking for postdoctoral positions to begin around January 2027. I am interested in studying statistical mechanics and soft matter, and particularly in active matter (turbulence, foams, wet systems, tissue mechanics), polymer physics (knots, stretching/rheology) and wetting and adhesion.
