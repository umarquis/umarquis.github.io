I was a PhD student in the [Mathematics department](https://www.maths.unitn.it/) of the University of Trento and in the [Complex Human Behavior Lab](https://chub.fbk.eu/) in FBK. I have also spent time at [CASA](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/bartlett/casa), in University College London. The main topic of my PhD was the study of urban expansion using tools of statistical mechanics, combining theoretical modeling, empirical analysis, and numerical simulations.

Looking forward, my interests are turned towards soft matter and biophysics. A non-exhaustive list of intriguing topics: bacterial suspensions, membranes, cell tissues, emulsions...
